# M7 soak, second session — a shorter outage plan for the candles-on window.
#
# Fewer and shorter than `plan-long.txt` on purpose. The question this session asks is
# different: not "does a session survive hours of breaks" (answered by the 1h44m
# candles-off run) but "does the candle feed change the answer, and does a *bar*
# subscription come back after a disconnect the way a quote subscription does".
#
# `BARS BUT NO CLOCK n` is the specific thing being hunted. It fires when a session is
# fed bars and nothing else, which is exactly the state a half-restored resubscribe
# would leave behind — candles back, quotes not. Breaks are therefore frequent enough
# to give the resubscribe many chances to go wrong, and short enough that the session
# spends most of the window with all four feeds live.
